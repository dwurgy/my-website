David Wurgler: style guide
Plain text. Paste anywhere.

The idea
One sentence on an empty page: "I make pretty things." Each word is a door, and the full stop is how you say hi. The brand is what's left out.

Names
- David Wurgler: me. The website, Threads, Ko-fi, and GitHub.
- David Wurgler Apps: the Discord server, and the umbrella over Firn, Utu, and Nynna.
- The apps keep their own identities. This brand frames them; it never restyles them.

The five rules
1. One typeface, one size, one weight.
2. Two tones and one dot.
3. Emptiness is the material. Never fill a gap because it's there.
4. Words before pictures.
5. The mark signs off.

Voice
- Plain and direct, like a person talking. Short sentences.
- No hype, no buzzwords, no emoji.
- Curly quotes and the serial comma.
- Say what a thing is and stop.
- Use only real facts. If a line needs a fact you don't have, ask.

Colour
            Light      Dark
  ground    #eff0ee    #0e0f10   (snow / night; always flat)
  ink       #0e0f10    #e9eae7   (text, and whatever is chosen)
  stone     #696b6a    #8e908e   (secondary text, and whatever isn't chosen)
  ember     #e5482d    #f0573b   (the dot; nothing else, ever)
Never set text in ember. Follow the system's light or dark setting; there is no theme button.

Type
- Averia Serif Libre Light, 21px (19px on phones), line height 1.5.
- No bold, no italic, no display sizes, no capitals for emphasis, no second typeface.
- Hierarchy comes from ink against stone, and from space.
- The favicon alone uses Regular.

The name
- "I make pretty things." is the wordmark on the site.
- "David Wurgler." is the wordmark everywhere else.
- "DW." is the short form, for profile pictures and small squares only.
- Each ends in the dot. One dot per surface, so the sentence and the wordmark never share one.
- In plain text, where the dot can't be drawn, write the name with no full stop.

The dot
- A drawn shape, a quarter of the letter height, on the baseline, just after the last letter.
- On screen it wobbles slowly. In stills it is one fixed, slightly uneven shape. Never a perfect circle, except in the favicon.
- It never appears by itself, and never as a bullet, a row, or a pattern.

The mark
- A ridge over still water. Its longer reflection is the W.
- It is the maker's stamp: once, small, at the end. Ridge in stone, W in ink, or one colour.
- Never a profile picture or a favicon, and never beside the name.
- Never redraw it.

Layout
- A great deal of empty space. More than feels correct.
- Pictures have 3px corners and nothing else: no shadows, borders, frames, or cards.
- Controls are plain words in stone that turn ink under the hand. No icons, no buttons.

Motion
- Smooth and short. Nothing makes you wait.
- With reduced motion on, everything simply changes.
