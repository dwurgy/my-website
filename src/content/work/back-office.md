I moved the company off QuickBooks Desktop and an unsupported in-house database onto Odoo 19, as the sole internal owner. It took ten weeks from kickoff to go-live, with zero downtime and every order shipped on launch day. It produced the company's first live P&L. Sales tax is automated across nine states, and month-end compliance went from three days to one report. Serial tracking with searchable test prints removed 500+ warehouse trips a year.
