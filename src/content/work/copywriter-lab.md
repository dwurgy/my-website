NW Copywriter Lab is an internal tool that turns a part number into an eBay title that doubles as a quote line, a product description, and weight and dimensions, and it saves the spec data for store filters. I prototyped it in Google AI Studio, rebuilt it with Claude, and deployed it on Cloudflare. It went from idea to production in three days.
