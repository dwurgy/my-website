The name, said OO-too, is Finnish for haze or mist. Utu writes the forecast as a sentence, like "Grey and mild. Showers drift in around 4 and stay through the evening." The numbers come second. It has soft, hazy skies, gentle motion, and widgets that answer questions directly: "Umbrella? Yes, after 4." Weather, without the noise.
