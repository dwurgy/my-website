Alfred answers questions from live data, such as which in-stock units meet a customer's license requirement. The SOP Assistant answers how-to questions from the company's own process docs. Both are built on the Gemini API and are in ongoing use across the team. A question that used to mean a walk to the warehouse is now one question.
