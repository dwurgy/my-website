You fly freely across one open world, liberate outposts, and hunt the giants that survived the flood. You can play it solo or with a friend. I'm building it on my own with Claude Code, in Unreal Engine 5.8 and C++. The current milestone is the flight prototype: hover, flight, heat, and two-player networking. The name is from Psalm 42:7, "Deep calls to deep." It fits the drowned world, and the music's main motif is planned around kulning, a Scandinavian herding call sung across valleys.
